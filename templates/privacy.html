Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, government identifiers, or any data beyond what is needed to provide the expense tracking service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate your Spendly account — to display your expenses, generate summaries, and provide the features of the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the service.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical precautions to protect it from unauthorised access, including hashed passwords and secure connections.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data brokers. The only external resources loaded by the app are web fonts from Google Fonts, which are subject to Google's own privacy policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the app. We will respond as promptly as possible.
